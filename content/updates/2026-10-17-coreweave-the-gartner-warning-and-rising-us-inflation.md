## 1. CoreWeave: what was the news?

CoreWeave’s stock declined sharply as investors became concerned about its high debt levels, massive data center investments, and how much of its future growth will need to be financed through borrowing. Yields on CoreWeave’s bonds also increased significantly, indicating that credit investors perceive a higher level of risk. The concerns are primarily about the financing and profitability of its AI infrastructure strategy, despite the company’s substantial backlog of contracted business.

## 2. The Gartner report: what was the news?

Research firm Gartner warned that more than 80% of so-called neocloud providers could cease to operate independently by 2030. According to Gartner, there is a potential mismatch between the massive investments being made in AI infrastructure and the revenue these AI services may ultimately generate, raising questions about whether the capital invested can earn an adequate return. This is a forecast about the sector as a whole, not an announcement that CoreWeave or Nebius is likely to disappear in the near term.

Source: [read the Gartner report](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-10-14-gartner-says-over-us-dollars-three-and-a-half-trillion-of-artificial-intelligence-infrastructure-spending-could-be-exposed-to-impairment-by-the-end-of-2030).

## 3. The US CPI report: what was the news?

US consumer prices increased by 3.7% year over year in September, up from 3.4% in August, while monthly inflation reached 0.6%. Higher gasoline and other energy costs were among the main contributors to the acceleration in inflation. This makes it more difficult for the Federal Reserve to cut interest rates and increases the possibility that financing costs will remain elevated for longer.

## 4. How are these three developments connected?

The Gartner report raises questions about whether AI infrastructure companies can generate sufficient returns on their enormous investments, while the CPI data creates uncertainty about future interest rates. This combination is particularly challenging for capital-intensive companies such as CoreWeave, which must invest and finance substantial infrastructure before receiving the full benefits of their investments. As a result, investors may sell stocks such as CoreWeave and Nebius even when there has been no new negative announcement about their underlying business operations.

## 5. Has anything fundamentally changed in the companies themselves?

The key distinction is between a deterioration in business performance and a deterioration in the risk outlook.

- **Operations:** these three developments do not, by themselves, indicate that CoreWeave’s contracts, revenue outlook, or day-to-day operations have deteriorated.
- **Financial position:** concerns about debt levels and financing costs are nevertheless relevant to CoreWeave. These risks should not be dismissed as merely market sentiment.
- **Strategy:** Gartner’s report questions the long-term sustainability of the sector’s business model, but it does not prove that CoreWeave or Nebius will be unable to build profitable businesses.

## Conclusion

The share price decline does not automatically mean that these companies are performing worse fundamentally. However, the risk outlook has changed: investors are becoming more critical of valuations, debt levels, financing costs, and the returns on AI infrastructure investments. To determine whether the underlying investment thesis has genuinely weakened, we need to assess the latest company results, contracts, revenue growth, cash flows, and financing requirements separately.
